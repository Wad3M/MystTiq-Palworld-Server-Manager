# v0.8.11.0 — Pal Positions on the Map

This is the second item of the user's automated run (remaining roadmap in reverse order). The v0.8.10.0 icon update was
inserted before it. The roadmap item: "Pal positions on the map".

## What the save really holds

Level.sav.json holds every owned Pal in `CharacterSaveParameterMap`, and each one has a `LastJumpedLocation`. Wild
Pals are not in the save. Read-only research on this project's real world showed that a Pal's saved position only
means "it is here" for some Pals, depending on the container its `SlotId` points at:

| Container | How it is recognised | Real save | Is its position real? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base workers | the `container_id` of a base's `WorkerDirector` (BaseCampSaveData) | 9 | Yes: 8 stand 15–46 m from their base, and 1 has no position |
| Party | a `CharacterContainerSaveData` entry with `SlotNum` 5 | 3 | Yes: where it last was with its owner |
| Palbox | `SlotNum` 960 | 65 | **No.** It is where the Pal was last let out, up to about 1 km from anything |

Separately from the container, some positions are unset:
- 25 are exact 0,0;
- 6–7 have a placeholder altitude at or above 900000;
- 2 are within 10 m of the world origin, for Pals that were never placed.

## What changed

- **`SavePalLocationReader`** (HeadlessHost, pure).
  - It classifies every Pal by its container: base worker, party (10 slots or fewer), Palbox, or unknown.
  - It returns base workers and party Pals that have a real position, with species, level, nickname and owner.
  - An alpha Pal (`BOSS_<id>`) is shown as its species plus "alpha".
  - It counts, but does not return, Palbox Pals, Pals without a position, and Pals whose container is unknown.
  - Property names are matched exactly, because the save has both `"ID"` and `"id": null` side by side. A
    case-insensitive lookup found the null one first during research.
- **The explorer snapshot** (`/api/v1/world/players-guilds`) gains `palLocations` and `palSummary`.
  - Each Pal is named by owner (from the player list) and by base guild.
  - A read failure becomes a warning and "no Pals". Guilds and bases are never lost.
- **The Map page** gets a Pals layer, calibrated Palpagos view only (like bases):
  - Violet (#9F7AEA) circles, clustered: Pals within 10 px at the current zoom share one circle with a count.
  - A cluster within 12 px of a base square is drawn as a **badge on the base's top-right corner**. At normal zoom,
    base workers otherwise sit exactly under the base square. The tooltip says so.
  - Clicking a circle or its row zooms all the way in (10×), where workers separate around their base.
  - Layer order is bases, then Pals, then players. Name labels now step around the Pal markers, using the new
    `obstacles` parameter of `MapLabelLayout.ComputeLabelOffsets`. The live check found a player's name printed over
    a badge's count.
  - A "Show Pals (at bases and in parties)" checkbox, a status line, and a "Pals on the map" list card.
  - The status line says what is drawn, and what is not and why: Palbox Pals, unset positions, and wild Pals.
- **Species** are the game's internal ids (PinkCat, Sheepball…), and the status line says so. English names are not
  in the save; that is the Give Item display-names item, still to come. Nothing is guessed.

## Verification

- **Logic harness, 2 scenarios.**
  - The reader on a fixture shaped like the real save, with base, party and Palbox containers, the `id`/`ID` trap,
    nested Level, and an alpha. It checks unset positions, unknown containers and an empty save.
  - The layer: clustering and its average position, titles, the tooltip cut at 12, the badge rule (the nearest base
    wins, a distant cluster stays put), label obstacles, and the status texts.
- **Route smoke** (`Test-v0.8.11.0-RouteSmoke.ps1`), on the published exe with its own FleetRoot and a synthetic
  save.
  - The route returns only the base worker (named by guild) and the party alpha (named by owner).
  - The summary counts the Palbox Pal and the two unset positions.
- **Real data, live Desktop** (clone profile, the same world). The status line reads: "11 Pal(s) on the map: 8
  working at bases, 3 in players' parties … Not drawn: 65 in a Palbox …, 9 without a recorded position."
  - At whole-map zoom there is a "10" badge on the base and one far party Pal.
  - Clicking the badge zooms to 10×.
  - A reader run on a temporary copy of the real save gave the same numbers. The copy was deleted afterwards.

## Limits

- Offline players logged off at the same base, so at the 10× maximum some Pal circles still sit under player rings.
  The tooltip and the list card name every Pal.
- Positions are the last the save recorded (the world saves periodically), not live. The game's REST API reports no
  Pal positions.
