# v1.0.7.3 — Loading Players and a Lighter Console (internal)

## Q-16 — The identity guard waits for a loading player (owner report 2026-10-11)

The report was "unable to connect with v1.0.7.1 from a remote computer". The evidence, all from 2026-10-08:
- **MystTiq's activity log:** the identity guard kicked Wade (`steam_76561197962020201`) at 18:12:25 and again at
  18:12:45, reporting "assigned no character (the new-character screen)" against his character 67D8D355.
- **PalDefender's session log:** he "connected" at 18:12:17 and 18:12:40, and "logged out" at 18:12:28 and 18:12:45.
  The first kick came 8 seconds after connecting.
- **AdminCommands' `connections.txt`:** "Unknown [UnknownID] has disconnected" both times. A normal join writes
  "Wade [67D8D355-…] has connected" only once the character has loaded (as on 2026-08-27).
- During loading, Palworld's REST `/players` lists the player with `playerId` "None".

**Not the cause:** the MystTiq API was never open to other computers (bind 127.0.0.1, sign-in off, unchanged since
before 2026-10-07). The guard's immediate reaction to "None" dates from v1.0.0.1.

**The fix:** `HeadlessIdentityGuardService` records when each online player was first seen with no character
(`noCharacterSince`). `IdentityGuard.NoCharacterIsDue` lets the guard act only after `LoadingGrace` (90 s).
- The entry is cleared when the player's own character appears, or when they leave.
- A different character is acted on at once, as before.
- `handledThisSession` still limits it to one kick per stay.

**Evidence:** the LogicHarness replays the owner's join. Listed as "None" at 8 s and 38 s, then his own character: no
kick, and nothing recorded. On a rejoin still "None" after 91 s: one kick, once per stay. A different character: kicked
at once. Owed: the owner joins through the v1.0.7.3 service with the kick on.

## Q-6 — The console does only the work that changed

Before: each 5-s poll cleared and refilled `LogLines` (500 rows) and `FilteredLogLines` (up to 500) item by item, even
when unchanged. It also rebuilt the Dashboard's activity lines and ran a regex per line. A line without a timestamp got
each poll's time.

After:
- `BulkObservableCollection.ReplaceAll` swaps the content with one Reset.
- `ApplyLogs` returns at once when the tail equals the last one applied.
- Untimed lines are stamped from `_lineFirstSeen`, the first time each was seen (pruned past 3,000 entries).
- The regex is compiled once.
- `ApplyConsoleFilter` runs only while the Console page is shown; otherwise the view is marked stale and brought up to
  date when the page opens or an export runs.

**Measured** (ArtworkHarness):
- An unchanged poll gives 0 list notifications (it was about 1,000); a changed one gives 2, one per list.
- 10,000 lines in 20 polls: 40 list updates in 77 ms, and the list stays at 500.
- A line's time is the same across polls.
- While the Console page is hidden its filtered view is untouched, and opening the page brings it up to date.

A 10-minute CPU and memory comparison on a live chatty server is owed.
