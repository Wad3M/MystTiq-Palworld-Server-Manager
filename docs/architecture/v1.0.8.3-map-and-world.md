# v1.0.8.3 — The Map and the World Pages (internal)

Roadmap C-3, using the mover from C-2 plus a short pre- and post-step for each page.

## MapViewModel (`vm.Map`)

**What moved:**
- the background and its presets;
- the viewport: zoom, pan, reset, and zoom to a point or a Pal group;
- the player, base and Pal markers and their label layout;
- the map's per-server readings (`_lastBaseLocations` and the rest) and `ApplyBaseLocations`;
- the Palpagos calibration switch;
- teleport points and the teleport-to-player commands.

**The window:** a tab switch used to clear the map's fields directly. It now calls `Map.ResetForNewServer()`, and the
fields are private again.

**The Players page** is a constructor parameter of the map, because the teleport commands and a click on a player
marker act on the selected player.

## WorldViewModel (`vm.World`)

**What moved:**
- the Inspector (players, guilds, bases and Pals from the world save) and the Pal editor;
- the Bases and Guilds pages, with their filters, exports and operations (transfer, recovery, ownership);
- character migration;
- world transactions;
- the guild-leader command.

**What stays with the window, and why:**
- `RefreshWorldExplorerAsync` and `RefreshPlayerGuildExplorerAsync` feed several pages: the world-file workspace, the
  world cards, the semantic-state line and the Players page's counts.
- `OpenSelectedGuildLeaderAsync` navigates to the Players page.

**What the page receives in its constructor:**
- the Map and Players pages;
- the window's refreshes as `Func<Task>` delegates: the two above, plus the operations, validation and Pals refreshes.

The delegates use the method names the page calls, so its code is unchanged. The session gained `IsConnected` (the
window's `ManagementApiConnected`) and `BusyReason`.

## Found while moving

**The role audit** follows code-behind handlers to the ViewModel methods they call. It looked only for `vm.Method(`, so
the Bases page's handlers (`vm.World.PreviewTransferBaseAsync(...)`) lost their roles and the code-behind role check
failed. It now looks through a page as well.

**`ToggleWorldMapCommand`** is bound nowhere; the world cards set the world-map expansion directly. It moved unchanged.
