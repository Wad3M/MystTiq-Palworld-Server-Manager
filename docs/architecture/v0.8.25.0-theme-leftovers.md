# v0.8.25.0 — Every Colour Follows the Mode, and Windows Contrast Themes

The last of the "smaller items". v0.8.16.0 added the Midnight and High contrast modes and Compact density, and left two
things undone:
- **the decorative colours** outside the theme catalogue (glows, `BoxShadow` shorthands, a few fixed borders and
  surfaces), which stayed at their Dark values in every mode;
- **Windows' own "Contrast themes"**, which MystTiq did not read.

## Decorative colours

**Every literal colour in the styles is now a resource.** That covers `Styles/DesignSystem.axaml`, `MainWindow.axaml`
and the two history charts, and nothing literal is left outside comments and keyed resources:
- 92 colours: borders, surfaces, text and drop-shadow glows;
- 21 whole shadow values, because a `BoxShadow` list cannot take a resource per colour, but the whole value can be one.

`DecorativePalette` holds each colour's Dark value and a role (border, background, text, glow). `ThemeApplier` writes
them for the current mode:

| Mode | Borders | Surfaces | Text | Glows and shadows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dark | exactly as tuned | exactly as tuned | exactly as tuned | exactly as tuned |
| Midnight | as Dark | dark ones deeper | as Dark | as Dark |
| Light | bright ones darker; dark ones toward the Light border | dark ones turn light, tinted by their hue | toward the Light text (4.5:1 on a card) | half as strong |
| High contrast | the palette's border (white) | dark ones become the page background | the palette's text | none |

A new mode needs no per-colour tuning. The three keyed gradients no mode reached are derived like their siblings:
- **WarningGlassGradient**, like its Primary, Success and Danger glass siblings;
- **RestoreGradient** and **RestoreGradientHover**, as orange-accent card surfaces.

The **history charts** (Dashboard and HOST tab) draw their background and grid as decorative colours, and their
series in the theme's own accents. They redraw when the theme changes.

**Found and fixed along the way:** on the Light palettes, success buttons (pale green) had white text at under 2:1.
They now use dark text there; Dark keeps white.

## Windows contrast themes

When Windows has a contrast theme on (Aquatic, Desert, Dusk, Night sky, or a custom one), `SystemContrastPalette`
reads its colours from Windows itself:
- `SystemParametersInfo(SPI_GETHIGHCONTRAST)` says whether one is on;
- `GetSysColor` gives its colours: window, window text, highlight, grey text, link, button face and button text.

How MystTiq uses them:
- **High contrast** uses those colours instead of MystTiq's own black and white:
  - surfaces take the window colour and text the window text;
  - borders and card borders take the window text;
  - the blue accent becomes the highlight, and the other accents the link colour;
  - buttons take the button face, stepped toward the button text on hover and press, with the theme's button text.
- **Follow the system** switches to High contrast while a contrast theme is on.
- **Dark, Light and Midnight** are explicit choices and ignore it.
- **A light contrast theme (Desert)** gets the light base palette and the day art, and the status colours come from
  the Light palette, so they stay readable on its light window.
- **Live changes:** a contrast theme switched on, off or changed while MystTiq is open is applied when Windows reports
  the change.
- **Elsewhere** (Linux, macOS), and with no contrast theme, there is none and MystTiq's own palette applies.

**Found by the first light contrast theme render:** the base button style's text was white. Under Desert, whose
buttons take its light button face, that was invisible. The button text is now a resource: white as before, or the
contrast theme's button text.

## The HOST tab's own art

The user supplied the HOST tab's page art and navigation icon (2026-09-25). Since v0.8.17.0 the tab had borrowed the
System art and the diagnostics icon.
- `Assets/Icons/icon-host.png`: 1254×1254, like the other navigation illustrations.
- `Assets/Artwork/page-art-host-dark.png` and `-light.png`: the same 1672×941 image for day and night, since one was
  supplied. A night version can replace the dark file later.
- `ArtworkCatalog` maps the Host page to `host`. The ArtworkHarness checks both files, the 27 navigation
  illustrations, and that HOST no longer shares the System art.

## Verification

- **ArtworkHarness** (542 checks, 19 new; 3 of them for the HOST art):
  - Dark keeps every decorative colour and shadow exactly;
  - Light: decorative text at 4.5:1, dark surfaces turned light, glows halved, the last three gradients follow, success
    button text readable;
  - High contrast: white borders and text, no glows or shadows;
  - a dark (Aquatic) and a light (Desert) contrast theme, with Windows stood in by the harness:
    - its colours used, button text at 4.5:1 on its buttons;
    - Desert changed while open: light palette, day art, text at 7:1, status colours readable;
    - Follow the system switches to it, Dark ignores it, and MystTiq's own palette returns without it.
- **Renders inspected:** Desert and Aquatic, before and after the button fix.

## Not done
