# v1.0.9.3 — Level-gap scans (internal)

Roadmap B-2 (scheduled by the owner 2026-10-12, D-11).

## The signal

MystTiq records playtime only while it watches (the player registry, accrued per tick and capped at 10 minutes a tick).
So it never judges a total level against total playtime: a veteran first seen at 60 would look impossible. It judges
the levels gained **since it first saw the player** against the playtime since, and, for a sudden jump after honest
play, the gain since the level before.

## What an honest player can gain

`LevelGapScan.AllowedGain(from, hours, expRate)` walks the bands from the starting level: 20 levels an hour from level
1, 10 from 10, 5 from 20, 3 from 30, 2 from 40, 1 from 50, 0.5 from 60, each times the experience rate (capped at 20).
That reaches level 50 in about 12 hours of play, several times faster than ordinary progression, on purpose: the rule is
for edited or cheated levels (dozens in minutes), not fast players. Gains within `SlackLevels` (3) never count.

## Calibration (the real world's backups)

Copies of the real world's 17 daily backups (2026-07-31 to 2026-10-08, one per day from `C:\GameServers\Palworld\Backups`)
were decoded with the app's own converter and each player's level read. The server runs at experience rate 1. Every real
gain fits the bands with room to spare: the largest, Wade from 9 to 15 in 17 hours of wall-clock time, is 12% of what the
bands allow for that time; playtime is at most the wall-clock time, and the same gain would only be reported in under 15
minutes of play. The world's players reach level 15 at most, so the higher bands are not exercised by real data; they stay
generous, adjustable (`rules.json`), flag-only, and a week of the owner's server with the rule on is carried (E-16).

## The service

`EvaluateLivePlayersAsync` (every automation tick): for each online player with a numeric level and registry playtime,
`LevelGapScan.Add` records a sample when the level changes (a lower level, a reset or a migrated character, starts the
history again; 50 samples kept, the first always) into `anticheat/level-history.json`, then `Evaluate`. A gap is
recorded through the existing `RecordAsync` with `AntiCheatResponse.Flag` (a finding, a notification, the activity log);
the cooldown key includes the level, so the same gain is not reported twice in 30 minutes. The experience rate is read
from `PalWorldSettings.ini` (`ExpRate=`) when a level changes.

## Evidence

- LogicHarness: the bands (level 1 to 10 in 27 minutes at most, one level an hour at 50, doubling at twice the rate,
  level 50 within about 12 hours), honest climbs and slack not reported, a veteran not reported, an edited jump and a
  sudden jump after honest play reported, a higher rate allowing more, the history rules, reading `ExpRate`.
- The service with a fake player list: level changes recorded and kept across a restart, an out-of-line gain reported
  with its numbers and a notification, no kick, nothing recorded once switched off.
- Live: carried (E-16).
