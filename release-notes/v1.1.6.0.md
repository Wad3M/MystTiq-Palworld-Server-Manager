# v1.1.6.0 — Sign In with Nexus Mods

## Nexus Mods: sign in instead of an API key

The Nexus Mods card (MODs > MOD Sources) no longer asks for a personal API key: Nexus Mods does not allow them in apps
any more. Press **Sign in with Nexus Mods**: Nexus's own page opens in your browser, you approve MystTiq there, and the
card shows "Signed in as …". MystTiq never sees your Nexus password.

- The sign-in is kept on this PC, encrypted for your Windows account (on Linux, until MystTiq closes), and is never sent
  to the MystTiq service. MystTiq renews it on its own while you use it.
- **Sign out** forgets it on this PC. To withdraw MystTiq's access completely, remove MystTiq on nexusmods.com under
  your account's authorised applications.
- A key saved by an earlier version is deleted from this PC the first time v1.1.6.0 starts.
- Everything else works as before: the lists, lookup by id or page address, Premium direct downloads, Mod Manager
  Download links for free accounts, and the MOD browser's Nexus Mods source.

Until Nexus Mods registers MystTiq for sign-in, the card says sign-in is not available yet; download MODs on
nexusmods.com and use Choose ZIP meanwhile.

## Upgrading from v1.1.5.1

Run the new setup, or extract the new ZIP or portable ZIP as before (a portable copy: move your `data` folder into it),
or use **Update** on the MystTiq row of the Update Center once this release is published. Nothing to convert. Then sign
in with Nexus Mods on the Nexus Mods card if you use it. Coming from an older version, read the [v1.1.5.1](v1.1.5.1.md) and
[v1.1.5.0](v1.1.5.0.md) notes first.

Back up server data before upgrading. Keep application files separate from server data; closing the desktop does not stop a managed server until you choose Exit from the tray.
