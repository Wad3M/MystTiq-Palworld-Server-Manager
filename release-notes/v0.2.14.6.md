# v0.2.14.6 — Transaction Center

## Added
- Read-only Transaction Center inside World Inspector.
- Durable journal discovery for world transactions and world imports.
- Discovery of character reset, character clone, and ownership transaction reports.
- Transaction status, target, duration, warning count, backup reference, report reference, and rollback-availability display.
- Search and state/operation filtering.
- Detailed stage and diagnostic inspection.
- Open Backup, Open Report, and Open Journals actions.

## Safety
- This release does not execute rollback operations.
- The Transaction Center only reads durable journals and reports stored outside the active world.
- Malformed or partially written records are skipped and logged rather than aborting the history refresh.
