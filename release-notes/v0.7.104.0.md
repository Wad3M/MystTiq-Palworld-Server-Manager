# v0.7.104.0 — Alert Center: A Pinned Alert Unpins Itself

Eighth of the "enhancements to features we already have". A gap named back in v0.7.102.0: a pinned Critical alert
stayed pinned forever, even after the "Resolved: ..." notice for it had already arrived.

- A pinned alert (Low disk space is the only rule pinned by default) now **unpins itself** the moment its
  condition clears — right alongside the existing "Resolved: ..." Success notice. The alert stays in your
  notification history; it just stops demanding attention.
- Switching the rule off while an alert is still pinned unpins it too. That path sends no "Resolved" notice (by
  design — nothing was actually fixed, the rule was just turned off), so this closes the one way a pinned alert
  could otherwise be stuck forever with no follow-up at all.
- Nothing else about alerting changed: still one alert per episode, still silent while the condition holds, still
  no re-alert inside the cooldown.
- Checked on two isolated sidecars (a forced low-disk Critical) and on the final published binary: recovery
  unpins, staying-off unpins, neither fabricates a notice the other doesn't already send.
- Not verified: the equivalent gap on the crash-recovery "server is DOWN, automatic recovery gave up" pinned
  notice — it isn't tracked the same way and is out of scope for this version (see the architecture doc).
