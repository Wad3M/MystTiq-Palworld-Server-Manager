# MystTiq Palworld Server Manager v0.2.15.5

## Centralized MOD Health & Identity — Phase 5

This phase removes independent MOD-health interpretations from the verification and dashboard paths and establishes `ModHealthEvaluationService` as the single health authority.

### Health rules

- **Healthy** — the MOD satisfies its type-specific verification requirements.
- **Active** — installed/enabled and ready, but a definitive verification pass has not yet been run.
- **Runtime Unverified** — UE4SS/Lua MOD is enabled and present in the Active Mods Root, but no matching UE4SS runtime load evidence is available.
- **Misconfigured** — UE4SS MOD is not present in the resolver-selected Active Mods Root.
- **Disabled** — installed but intentionally disabled.
- **Missing / Failed / Attention** — explicit installation, runtime, duplicate, or state-reconciliation problems.

PAK and ordinary Workshop packages do not require a `Starting Lua mod` UE4SS log entry.

### Workshop identity

Installed Workshop items now reuse MystTiq's existing cached Steam metadata during inventory scans. A known title such as `PalSchema (3625280368)` therefore remains stable across refresh, Verify All, dashboard rebuilds, and offline Steam metadata periods.
