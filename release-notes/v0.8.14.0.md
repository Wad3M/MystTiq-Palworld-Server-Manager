# v0.8.14.0 — Cards Follow the Signed-In Role

- **People signed in with a limited role see a cleaner app.** Cards their role can't use are now hidden instead of
  shown greyed out. Cards they can read but not change are shown read-only.
  - An **Operator** doesn't see user accounts, principals, Clone World, the Discord bot, mute and pause, or the
    migration and give tools.
  - A **Viewer** also doesn't see Fleet Actions, Teleport Points or the item finder.
- **A note says so.** When something is hidden, a line at the top explains it's because of the role.
- **Fixed:** Fleet Actions (back up, check or update every server) is available to Operators, as the server always allowed.
  Its buttons also stayed greyed out even when connected, for everyone; that's fixed too.
- Using MystTiq on this PC without signing in is unchanged: everything is available.
