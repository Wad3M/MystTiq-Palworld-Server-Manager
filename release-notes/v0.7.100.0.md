# v0.7.100.0 — Map: Zoom, Click-to-Zoom, and Last-Known Player Positions

Two requests on the Map page: scroll-wheel zoom with click-a-marker-to-zoom, and showing where people are with
online/offline told apart.

- **Scroll to zoom** toward the cursor (up to 10x); the page does not scroll while the wheel is over the map.
  **Drag to pan.** A zoom readout and a Reset View button sit above the controls.
- **Click a marker or a name to zoom in on it**: player dots, base squares, and both lists. A player click still
  selects that player for Teleport To Me / To Player.
- **Offline players are now on the map**, as hollow grey rings at the last position the save recorded for them;
  online players stay solid green dots at their live position. The lists mark each player `●` online or `○`
  offline with their in-game coordinates, tooltips show when the save was written, and a switch hides offline
  players. With the server stopped you can now see where everyone last was.
- On the real world all five players' last known positions are within a few map units of the game's start
  plateau, which also confirms the v0.7.96.0 map calibration.
- Last known is approximate: it is where the game last recorded the character (its last jump location), not a
  live position, and it says so.
- Not done: labels of players standing together still overlap (the list picks one by name); the map image gets
  soft beyond about 2x zoom.
- Checked live on the running app (window rendered and driven with input messages). Not verified: solid green
  online dots, since nobody was online.
