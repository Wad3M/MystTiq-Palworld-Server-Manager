# v0.8.3.0 — Give Item Picker

- **Find items and Pals by name instead of typing internal ids.** A new "Find an item or Pal" box under Player
  Administration searches the ids your server actually uses. Type "sphere mega" and it finds `PalSphere_Mega`.
- **The ids come from your own world save.** Every item and Pal in your world, plus the ids in your Starter Kits and
  ones you've given before. So they match your installed game version exactly, with nothing guessed. Each entry shows
  where it comes from (for example "in world ×757", "in a kit", "given before", "incl. alpha").
- **One click adds it.** Add to Give puts `item PalSphere_Mega 10` in the Give box. Add to Selected Kit puts it in the
  kit you're editing. Pals take a level (1–100) instead of an amount.
- Items nobody in your world has had yet aren't listed, and the page says so. You can still type any id by hand.
- Checked with 5 new logic-harness scenarios and a new route smoke test on the published build. The smoke includes a
  19 MB save read in 0.3 seconds. Also checked against your real world (122 items, 29 Pal species; read only) and by
  using it in the live app.
