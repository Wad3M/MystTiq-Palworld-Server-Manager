# v0.8.9.0 — Crash Reports in the Crash Analyzer

- **The Crash Analyzer now reads the game's own crash reports.** Unreal writes a report for every crash, with the
  exact error, and these are now part of every analysis. On your server it found three: two memory access violations
  and one engine fatal error.
- **The engine fatal error gets its own finding.** It's the "Trying to resize TArray to an invalid size" error from
  your server's 16 September crash. The finding says what the report shows and what to try, without guessing a cause
  it can't know.
- **New crash reports send an alert.** When a new report appears with a new critical problem, you get an alert naming
  the report and the likely cause. This includes crashes that happened while MystTiq wasn't running. Old reports are
  not announced when you upgrade.
- Mute and the crash-alert on/off switch are respected, and a crash already announced by crash recovery isn't
  announced twice.
- Checked with new tests and a new end-to-end test, and against copies of your server's three real crash reports.
