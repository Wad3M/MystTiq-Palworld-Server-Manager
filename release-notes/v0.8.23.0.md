# v0.8.23.0 — Every Button Follows the Role

- **Signed-in users only see what they can use.** When you sign in to a remote MystTiq, every button your role cannot
  use is now greyed out: on the pages, in right-click menus and on the Ribbon. That covers, for example, mod
  enable/disable, backup delete/restore, whitelist and alert settings, and the player, base and guild edits. Before,
  those buttons looked usable and MystTiq refused when pressed.
- Using MystTiq on this PC without signing in is unchanged: everything is available.
