# v0.8.6.0 — The Ribbon in Every Language

- **The Ribbon is now translated.** All its buttons and group titles appear in German or Spanish when you choose that
  language in Settings. Your Ribbon icons stay exactly as they are.
- **Fixed:** on the UE4SS and Doctor pages, the last Ribbon buttons could slide under the page header and be cut off,
  even in English. They now move into the "»" overflow menu when there isn't room, in every language.
- English reads exactly as before.
- Checked by visiting every page in English, German and Spanish, at the smallest and a large window size, looking for
  untranslated text and cut-off buttons.
