# v0.7.99.0 — Dedicated Map Page Under World

- New **Map** page in the World group (after Guilds), with its own icon. The live map used to be a card on
  the Players page; it moved here, and Players now has an Open Map button.
- Map first: the map is on the left and fits the window; presets, the real-world-positions switch, the
  players list (click a name to select; Teleport To Me / To Player) and a Guild bases list are beside it.
- Lists and tooltips show the in-game coordinates the game's own map shows, e.g. "MystTik (248, -495)".
- **Fixed: every map marker was drawn in the top-left corner** (since the markers were first built). They now
  land on their real position.
- **Fixed: markers were about 9 map units east of their point** because the shape was centred in the label
  under it. The shape's centre is now on the point.
- With both fixed, the Default Server's bases show on the start peninsula and the southern island, as the
  v0.7.96.0 calibration predicted.
- First version verified by looking at the running app: the Map page, the v0.7.98.0 Doctor findings and the
  v0.7.97.0 Crash Analyzer page were captured and checked on screen.
- Not verified on screen: player dots (no player was online) and the Crash Analyzer detail pane with data.
