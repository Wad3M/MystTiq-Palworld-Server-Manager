# v0.8.4.0 — Pause Discord, Email & Webhooks

- **Pause the pings, keep the record.** A new card in Alert Center pauses sending to Discord, email and webhooks for 1
  hour, 8 hours, 24 hours or 7 days. Every alert and notification still appears on the Notifications page, so nothing
  is lost. Use it for maintenance or quiet hours.
- **Resume** any time. Pauses end on their own, capped at 30 days. Notifications from the pause aren't sent
  afterwards, so there's no flood of stale messages when it ends.
- **Send Test Notification** checks your Discord, email or webhook setup, and tells you where the test went.
- Each send skipped during a pause is listed in the Activity log.
- Unlike Mute Alerts, which stops alerts entirely, this only stops the outside sends.
- Checked with 2 new logic-harness scenarios and a new route smoke test on the published build. Both use a real
  webhook receiver, which got nothing while paused and got notifications again after resuming. Also checked in the
  live app on the clone server.
