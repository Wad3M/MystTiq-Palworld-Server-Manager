# MystTiq v0.7.54.0 — Per-Page Title Background Artwork

## Highlights

- **The Home and World page headers now have real illustrated background artwork** — dark
  navy/pastel fantasy landscape banners matching the app's existing visual style, with Light-mode
  variants that switch automatically with your theme setting.
- **Server, Backups, Mods, Tools, and System don't have artwork yet.** This isn't a bug — the free
  image-generation service used to produce these hit its daily limit after 4 images. The other 5
  will follow in a future version once more can be generated.

## Upgrade notes

- No configuration changes. Purely additive — four new image assets and four new bindings.
- No way to visually render this app in the environment that built it — please open the Home and
  World pages in both Dark and Light mode before considering this fully verified.
- The shipped artwork was generated via a free, anonymous AI image-generation session (Bing Image
  Creator), not purchased/licensed stock art — worth being aware of before wide distribution.
